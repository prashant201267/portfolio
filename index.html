About Me

A quick introduction

I'm a software engineer with a passion for building efficient, user-friendly applications. I enjoy solving complex problems, writing clean code, and collaborating with clients to turn their vision into reality.

I’m a backend-focused Software Developer with 3+ years of experience working across product development, production support, automation, and AI-driven solutions. I currently work as a Product Solution Engineer at PhonePe, where I work on insurance backend systems, handle critical production issues and RCA, build features using Java and Spring Boot, and develop Python-based automation to improve operational efficiency. What makes me unique is that I enjoy working at the intersection of backend engineering, automation, and AI—I don’t just focus on implementing features, but also look for ways to simplify processes, automate repetitive work, and solve real operational problems. I’m comfortable taking ownership of a problem from understanding the requirement and debugging the issue to designing, implementing, and improving the solution.
